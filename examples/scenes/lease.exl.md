# What does Leo commit to if he signs this lease?

<brief form="annotated">
<audience>People renting their first flat; they have never read a lease to the end</audience>
<question>What does Leo commit to if he signs this lease, and should he sign it as it is?</question>
<example>Leo Park rents the flat at 12 Rose Street for 900 euros a month</example>
<misconception>Believing the deposit is a fee that the landlord keeps</misconception>
<check>Predict the most the rent can be after the first renewal</check>
<terms>deposit, normal wear</terms>
</brief>

<theme name="business" />

Leo Park is about to rent the flat at 12 Rose Street. Six clauses fit on one page; four of them decide what he pays, and when.

<annotated id="clauses" title="Four clauses decide what Leo pays, and when" arc="example" ref="lease.md#Clauses">
<passage>
2. Rent. The rent is 900 euros a month, paid by the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.

3. Deposit. The tenant pays a deposit of 1,800 euros, equal to 2 months of rent. The landlord returns it within 30 days of the end of the lease, less the cost of any damage beyond normal wear.

4. Repairs. Small repairs up to 100 euros are paid by the tenant. Larger repairs, and anything due to age or normal wear, are paid by the landlord.

5. Renewal. At renewal the rent may rise by at most 5 %.
</passage>
<note quote="900 euros a month, paid by the 5th day" label="Clause 2">Every month, by the 5th: 900 euros by bank transfer.</note>
<note quote="1,800 euros, equal to 2 months of rent" label="Clause 3">On day one, two months of rent on top of the first month.</note>
<note quote="returns it within 30 days of the end of the lease" label="Clause 3">That money comes back within 30 days of leaving.</note>
<note quote="Small repairs up to 100 euros are paid by the tenant" label="Clause 4">A dripping tap is Leo's; a broken boiler is not.</note>
<note quote="rise by at most 5 %" label="Clause 5">After a year, the rent can go up, but by 5 % at most.</note>
</annotated>

Two words of clause 3 carry most of its weight.

<concept term="Deposit" ref="lease.md#Deposit">
<define>Money the tenant hands over at the start, which the landlord holds only to pay for damage, and returns at the end.</define>
<analogy>The deposit on a glass bottle: you get it back when you bring the bottle back unbroken.</analogy>
<example>Leo pays 1,800 euros on 1 March and gets them back within 30 days of leaving, less any damage.</example>
</concept>

<concept term="Normal wear" ref="lease.md#Repairs">
<define>The ageing a flat goes through when it is lived in with care: faded paint, a worn floor.</define>
<analogy>A car's tyres wear down even when it is driven well.</analogy>
<example>Paint faded by the sun is normal wear; a hole in the wall from a shelf is damage.</example>
</concept>

<compare id="deposit-vs-fee" title="The deposit comes back; a fee does not" a="A fee" b="The deposit of clause 3" ref="lease.md#Deposit" arc="contrast">
<pair label="Who keeps it" a="the landlord, for good" b="the landlord, only while the lease lasts" />
<pair label="When it comes back" a="never" b="within 30 days of the end" winner="b" />
<pair label="What it can pay for" a="anything" b="only damage beyond normal wear" winner="b" />
</compare>

<argument id="sign" title="Leo can sign as it is, with one precaution" ref="lease.md#Clauses" arc="answer">
<claim>Leo can sign this lease as it is</claim>
<support label="The rent is capped">At renewal it can rise by 5 % at most, so the second year costs at most 945 euros a month.</support>
<support label="Big repairs are not his">Anything over 100 euros, and everything due to age, is paid by the landlord.</support>
<objection label="2,700 euros on day one" reply="The 1,800 of the deposit come back within 30 days of leaving.">The first month and the deposit together are three months of rent at once.</objection>
<objection label="Leaving early costs a month" reply="Only if he leaves before the year ends, and with one month of notice.">Clause 6 charges one month of rent as compensation.</objection>
<verdict>Sign, and photograph the flat on day one, so normal wear is not charged as damage</verdict>
</argument>

<quiz id="renewal-check" question="Leo renews after the first year. What is the most his rent can be?">
<option correct="true" why="Clause 5 caps the rise at 5 %, and 5 % of 900 is 45.">945 euros a month</option>
<option why="The rent may rise at renewal; clause 5 only caps by how much.">900 euros a month, since it cannot change</option>
<option why="That is a 10 % rise, twice what clause 5 allows.">990 euros a month</option>
</quiz>
