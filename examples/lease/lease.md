# Lease of the flat at 12 Rose Street, second floor

Between Marta Ruiz (the landlord) and Leo Park (the tenant). Signed on 1 March 2026. A fictional lease, for the example scene `examples/scenes/lease.exl.md`.

1. Term. The lease runs for 1 year from 1 March 2026 and renews by itself for 1 more year unless either side gives notice 2 months before the end.

2. Rent. The rent is 900 euros a month, paid by the 5th day of each month by bank transfer.

3. Deposit. The tenant pays a deposit of 1,800 euros, equal to 2 months of rent. The landlord returns it within 30 days of the end of the lease, less the cost of any damage beyond normal wear.

4. Repairs. Small repairs up to 100 euros are paid by the tenant. Larger repairs, and anything due to age or normal wear, are paid by the landlord.

5. Renewal. At renewal the rent may rise by at most 5 %.

6. Leaving early. The tenant may leave before the end of the term by giving 1 month of notice and paying 1 month of rent as compensation.
